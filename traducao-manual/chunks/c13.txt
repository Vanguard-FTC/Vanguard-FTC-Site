s1107	The ⟦0⟧third period of each MATCH is 2 minutes (2:00) long and called the teleoperated period (TELEOP). During TELEOP, DRIVERS remotely operate ROBOTS⟦/0⟧ to score points. See Table 9‑1 for detailed MATCH timing.
