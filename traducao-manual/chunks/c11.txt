s0927	The ARENA is modular and is assembled, used, disassembled, and transported many times during the competition season. It undergoes wear and tear. Every effort is made to ensure that ARENAS are consistent from event to event.
s0928	However, ARENAS are assembled in different venues by different event staff and volunteers, with some regions facing unique challenges resulting in some small variations.
s0929	⟦0⟧The ARENA specifications are designed to accurately reflect the variations which may be present in official play, while still ensuring consistency of critical items. Contact your ⟦/0⟧⟦1⟧local support⟦/1⟧⟦2⟧ to request more information. ⟦/2⟧

s0931	⟦0⟧⟦/0⟧⟦1⟧⟦/1⟧⟦2⟧⟦/2⟧⟦3⟧9.2 FIELD⟦/3⟧
s0932	Each FIELD for BIOBUZZ is ⟦0⟧an approximately 144 in. by 144 in. (365.75 cm by 365.75 cm) area bounded by the inside surface of the walls⟦/0⟧ of the FIELD perimeter. The ⟦1⟧flooring surface of the FIELD is made of 36 interlocking soft foam TILES⟦/1⟧ which are each approximately 24 in. by 24 in. by 0.59 in. (60.95 cm by 60.95 cm by 1.50 cm) nominally sized.
s0933	The FIELD is populated with and surrounded by the following FIELD elements:
s0934	- 1 HIVE Structure, consisting of 1 Frame with 2 mounted HIVES (1 HIVE per ALLIANCE)
s0935	- 4 FLOWERS
s0936	Official events use the full BIOBUZZ FIELD manufactured and sold by AndyMark (am-5850_Full) or officially licensed equivalent.
s0937	The soft foam TILE surface of the FIELD is ⟦0⟧⟦1⟧FIRST⟦/1⟧ Tech Challenge Field Soft Tiles⟦/0⟧ (am-2499) or equivalent.
s0938	The primary version of the FIELD perimeter is the ⟦0⟧⟦1⟧FIRST⟦/1⟧ Tech Challenge Perimeter Kit⟦/0⟧ (am-0481) sold by AndyMark. All illustrations in this manual show the am-0481 version of the FIELD design. Other versions of the FIELD perimeter of similar functionality may also be used in competitions.
s0939	Some events, including the ⟦0⟧FIRST⟦/0⟧ Championship (see Section ⟦1⟧15.2⟦/1⟧⟦2⟧ ⟦/2⟧⟦3⟧Game Modification⟦/3⟧), will place the FIELDS on platforms or risers such that the FIELD is raised while the ALLIANCE AREAS remain at ground level.
s0940	The ⟦0⟧Event Field Setup Guide⟦/0⟧ allows events to optionally anchor the FIELD perimeter in place. Events using the AndyMark Perimeter Kit that choose to anchor their FIELD in place do not need to install the Perimeter Straps that are part of the AndyMark Perimeter.
s0941	The FIELD variant used at an event will be determined by the local Program Delivery Partner, and all competition FIELDS at the same event must comply with Section ⟦0⟧9.1⟦/0⟧⟦1⟧ ⟦/1⟧⟦2⟧Dimensions and Accuracy⟦/2⟧ and be consistent with each other per ⟦3⟧T404⟦/3⟧.

s0943	FIELD areas, zones, and markings of consequence are described below.
s0944	- The term “zone” is used to identify spaces within the FIELD.
s0945	- The term “area” is used to describe spaces outside of the FIELD.
s0946	Unless otherwise specified, the tape used to mark lines and zones throughout the FIELD can be either 1 in. (2.50 cm) wide or 2 in. (5.10 cm) wide ⟦0⟧ProGaff⟦1⟧®⟦/1⟧ Premium Professional Grade Gaffer Tape⟦/0⟧, or comparable gaffers tape in red, and electric blue. Areas outside the FIELD may be marked with other types or widths of tape, depending on the event.
s0947	The tape used to mark lines and zones throughout the FIELD is shown as continuous strips in all official specifications. While in some games the tape lines are permitted to be applied in non-continuous segments, this is not necessary this season. On the BIOBUZZ FIELD, none of the tape lines span across TILE seams. Events are encouraged to apply the tape lines continuously as shown in the specifications.

s0964	The HIVE Structure is located in the center of the FIELD. A frame holds a red HIVE and a blue HIVE. Each HIVE consists of 2 CELLS, one on either end of each HIVE. Each CELL is ⟦0⟧a three-dimensional structure that can hold NECTAR and POLLEN⟦/0⟧.

On the bottom face of each CELL is a unique AprilTag Cluster containing 4 distinct AprilTags.

The frame dimensions are shown in Figure 9‑8.
s0968	A panel is attached to each side of the frame with the BIOBUZZ logo. This graphic may not be present at all events.
