s0535	⟦0⟧⟦/0⟧⟦1⟧E505 ⟦/1⟧⟦2⟧*Only small benchtop machinery is permitted in team pits.⟦/2⟧ Small machinery is allowed but floor standing power tools are prohibited. The Event Director may impose additional limitations.

s0543	⟦0⟧⟦/0⟧⟦1⟧E510 ⟦/1⟧⟦2⟧*Don’t heat or cool ROBOT components to gain an advantage.⟦/2⟧ Teams may not attempt to gain a competitive advantage by heating or cooling ROBOT parts above or below the ambient temperature of the venue.
s0544	The intent of this rule is that ROBOTS should compete as if they were left alone for an extended period of time in the competition venue at ambient temperature. Behaviors that are against the spirit of this rule include, but are not limited to:
s0545	- ⟦0⟧Cooling fuses so they take longer to trip than normal,⟦/0⟧
s0546	- ⟦0⟧Heating batteries to increase their performance, and⟦/0⟧
s0547	- ⟦0⟧Freezing heatsinks so they are more effective at cooling other components.⟦/0⟧
s0548	Examples of behaviors that would not violate this rule include, but are not limited to:
s0549	- ⟦0⟧Heating or cooling components for reasons unrelated to ROBOT performance (e.g., warming up heat shrink tubing during installation)⟦/0⟧
s0550	- ⟦0⟧Using a battery that is slightly above ambient temperature as a result of its regular charging cycle, and⟦/0⟧
s0551	- ⟦0⟧Using a ROBOT with components that are warm as a result of prior use.⟦/0⟧
s0552	⟦0⟧⟦/0⟧⟦1⟧E511 ⟦/1⟧⟦2⟧*Charge batteries in a safe and fair manner.⟦/2⟧ When charging batteries teams must:
s0553	A. Charge batteries at a safe rate, following all manufacturer recommendations,
s0554	B. Never charge batteries on a battery charger that exceeds a 3-amp average channel current, and
s0555	C. Charge batteries using safe connectors.
s0556	Safe connectors are those with a polarized connector corresponding to the connector on the battery itself. Batteries must never be charged using alligator clips or similar.
