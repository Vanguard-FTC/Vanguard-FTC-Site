s1764	⟦0⟧⟦/0⟧⟦1⟧⟦/1⟧⟦2⟧⟦/2⟧⟦3⟧⟦/3⟧⟦4⟧12.4 ROBOT SIGN Rules⟦/4⟧
s1765	A ROBOT SIGN is ⟦0⟧a required assembly which attaches to the ROBOT⟦/0⟧. A ROBOT SIGN ⟦1⟧simultaneously identifies a ROBOT’S team number as well as its ALLIANCE affiliation⟦/1⟧ for FIELD STAFF. Criteria used in writing these rules include the following:
s1766	- Maximize FIELD STAFF’S ability to determine team number and ALLIANCE of a ROBOT,
s1767	- Minimize the amount of design challenge in creating ROBOT SIGNS, and
s1768	- Increase consistency in displaying ROBOT identification.
s1769	A template ROBOT SIGN is available for download in ⟦0⟧US Letter⟦/0⟧ and ⟦1⟧A4⟦/1⟧ size.
s1770	⟦0⟧⟦/0⟧⟦1⟧R401 ⟦/1⟧⟦2⟧*Minimum of two ROBOT SIGNS per ROBOT.⟦/2⟧⟦3⟧⟦4⟧ ⟦/4⟧⟦/3⟧⟦5⟧⟦6⟧ROBOT SIGNS ⟦/6⟧⟦/5⟧must be placed in at least 2 separate locations on the ROBOT. These locations must be on opposite or adjacent surfaces of the ROBOT, ≥90 degrees apart. All ROBOT surfaces visible to FIELD STAFF can be used for placing ROBOT SIGNS including the top of the ROBOT. ROBOT SIGNS must meet the following criteria:
s1771	A. be made of a robust material,
s1772	B. minimally be 6.5 inches (16.5 cm) wide,
s1773	C. minimally be 2.5 inches (6.4 cm) tall (Figure 12‑7), and
s1774	D. be supported by the structure/frame of the ROBOT.
s1775	The intent of this rule is for FIELD STAFF to easily view ROBOT SIGNS from at least 12 feet (3.65 meters) away before, during, and after the MATCH.
s1776	ROBOT SIGNS should be robust enough to withstand the rigors of MATCH play, but there is no specific guidance on what is robust enough. Teams should make a good faith effort with whatever they have available.
s1777	⟦0⟧⟦/0⟧⟦1⟧⟦/1⟧⟦2⟧⟦/2⟧⟦3⟧⟦/3⟧⟦4⟧Figure ⟦/4⟧12‑7: Team Number ROBOT SIGN Sizing
s1778	⟦0⟧⟦/0⟧⟦1⟧⟦/1⟧⟦2⟧R402 ⟦/2⟧⟦3⟧*ROBOT SIGNS indicate your ALLIANCE⟦/3⟧⟦4⟧⟦5⟧. ⟦/5⟧⟦/4⟧Each ROBOT SIGN must contain a rectangle with a solid red or blue opaque background at least 6.5 in. by 2.5 in. (16.50 cm by 6.35 cm) in size to indicate their ALLIANCE color (Figure 12‑7), as assigned in the MATCH schedule at the event. Visible markings on ROBOT SIGNS when installed on the ROBOT, other than the following, are prohibited:
s1779	A. those required per ⟦0⟧R403⟦/0⟧,
s1780	B. small amounts of hook-and-loop tape, hard fasteners, or functional equivalents,
s1781	C. narrow areas of differing colors exposed at corners, folds, or cutouts,
s1782	D. narrow markings on background solely for template purposes, and
s1783	E. cannot be powered or rely on power from any sources to illuminate/reveal ALLIANCE color.
s1784	ROBOT SIGNS that are reversible or configurable must not allow the opposite ALLIANCE color to be visible to FIELD STAFF, except where permitted by this rule.
