s0501	⟦0⟧⟦/0⟧⟦1⟧E117 ⟦/1⟧⟦2⟧*Enter only 1 ROBOT in the tournament.⟦/2⟧ At a given ⟦3⟧FIRST⟦/3⟧ Tech Challenge event, each team may only inspect and play MATCHES with 1 ROBOT. ⟦4⟧FIRST⟦/4⟧ Tech Challenge teams may only participate in 1 concurrent event at a time.

s0521	⟦0⟧⟦/0⟧⟦1⟧⟦/1⟧⟦2⟧⟦/2⟧⟦3⟧5.5 Pits⟦/3⟧
s0522	A team pit is the designated space, varying in size but typically no larger than a 10 ft. by 10 ft. by 10 ft. (~3 m x 3 m x 3 m) area, where a team may work on their ROBOT. s0523	The pit area refers to the general area where team pits are located which encompasses the aisles between the pits, pit admin, ROBOT inspection, practice FIELD, or other areas where ROBOTS may be active or worked on. All pit rules apply to the full pit area.
s0524	Additional limitations beyond those listed below may be imposed by the Event Director but they should be clearly communicated at least 48 hours before the event start time and applied to all teams fairly.
s0525	Team pits may or may not have a table and power outlet. If individual team outlets are not provided, the venue must provide access to team-usable outlets in the pit area for charging batteries. Power may not be available overnight for a multi-day event.
